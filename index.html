Data sources & how this works +

Species eligibility and individual learnable moves come from Pokémon Showdown's Champions M-C format validator. League-specific bans are not applied. Species and item clauses, moves and point budgets are checked for export; complete battle validation still belongs in Showdown.

All league information is saved in this browser's local storage. Back up your league to move devices or protect it from browser-data deletion. Nothing is uploaded when you use this planner. Most sprites load from Pokémon Showdown. Missing newer Mega forms use bundled Champions artwork; the planner and data also work without remote images.
